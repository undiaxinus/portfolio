# Interview Prep for IT Developer

Base sa screenshot, mukhang `IT Developer` role ito at virtual interview sa Google Meet. Pinaka-safe ngayon ay ihanda mo ang `self-intro`, `common questions`, at `quick answers` mo.

## Unang Sasabihin

Kapag pinakilala ka, puwede mong sabihin ito:

> Good morning, thank you for the opportunity. My name is Jam, and I’m a full-stack developer with experience in Angular, Tailwind CSS, PHP, Supabase, PostgreSQL, and MySQL. I enjoy building clean, responsive, and user-friendly web applications. I’m especially interested in roles where I can contribute both on the frontend and backend, continue learning, and help create practical solutions for users.

Mas natural na version:

> Good morning po. I’m Jam, a full-stack developer. Ang experience ko po ay mainly sa Angular, Tailwind CSS, PHP, Supabase, at SQL databases like PostgreSQL and MySQL. Mahilig po akong gumawa ng responsive at malinis na web applications, at gusto ko pong makahanap ng role where I can contribute, grow, and learn from a strong team.

## Common Questions

### Tell me about yourself.
Sagot: short intro, tech stack, anong type ng projects, at bakit ka fit sa role.

### What are your strengths?
Sample:

> One of my strengths is adaptability. I can work on both frontend and backend tasks depending on project needs. I also focus on writing clean and maintainable code, and I’m willing to learn quickly when new tools or requirements come in.

### What are your weaknesses?
Sample:

> Sometimes I spend extra time making sure details are polished, especially in UI work. But I’ve been improving by managing priorities better and focusing first on functionality before refinement.

### Why do you want to join our company?
Sample:

> I want to join your company because I’m looking for a role where I can apply my current development skills while continuing to grow professionally. I’m also interested in working with a team where I can contribute to real projects and gain broader industry experience.

### Why should we hire you?
Sample:

> You should hire me because I have practical experience in both frontend and backend development, and I’m eager to contribute immediately while continuing to learn. I’m responsible, adaptable, and focused on building solutions that are both functional and user-friendly.

## Technical Questions Na Puwedeng Lumabas

### Ano ang gamit mo sa frontend?
- `Angular 17`
- `Tailwind CSS`
- component-based structure
- reusable UI

### Ano ang gamit mo sa backend?
- `PHP`
- APIs
- database integration
- form/data handling

### Anong databases ang alam mo?
- `PostgreSQL`
- `MySQL`
- `Supabase`

### Paano mo sini-secure ang user data?
Sample:

> I avoid hardcoding credentials, use environment configuration, sanitize inputs, validate data on both frontend and backend, and use secure password hashing like Argon2 when applicable.

### How do you handle deadlines or pressure?
Sample:

> I handle pressure by breaking tasks into smaller priorities, communicating clearly if there are blockers, and focusing first on the most important deliverables so I can stay productive under deadlines.

## Kung Tanungin Ka Tungkol Sa Projects

Gamitin mo itong format:
- `Project name`
- `Ano ang goal`
- `Ano ang role mo`
- `Tech stack`
- `Biggest challenge`
- `Result`

Sample:

> One project I worked on involved building a responsive web application using Angular and Tailwind CSS for the frontend, with PHP and database integration on the backend. My role was to develop UI components, connect forms to the backend, and help organize the data flow. One challenge was keeping the interface clean and responsive while making sure the backend validations were correct. The result was a more usable and maintainable application.

## Mga Dapat Mong Ihanda

- Stable internet
- Quiet place
- Earphones or headset
- Open portfolio/GitHub/CV
- Listahan ng projects mo
- Pen at notes
- 2-minute self-intro practice

## Questions Na Ikaw Ang Magtatanong

Maganda kung may tanong ka rin sa dulo:
- `What technologies does your development team currently use?`
- `What would success look like in the first 3 months for this role?`
- `Will this role focus more on frontend, backend, or full-stack tasks?`
- `What is the team setup and development workflow?`

## Quick Tips

- Sagot mo dapat `short, direct, confident`
- Huwag masyadong mahaba ang explanation
- Kapag hindi mo alam ang sagot:

> I haven’t worked on that deeply yet, but I’m willing to learn and I usually adapt quickly when needed.

- Smile
- Eye contact sa camera
- Magsalita nang malinaw

## Pinaka-Importanteng Script

- Intro
- Strengths
- Why hire you
- Project explanation
- Why do you want this role
